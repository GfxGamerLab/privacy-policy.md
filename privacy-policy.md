# Privacy Policy for Spider GFX Tool

**Effective Date:** October 6, 2026

Spider GFX Tool ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Spider GFX Tool mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our app.

### Information Collection and Use
Our application is designed as a system optimization and gaming utility tool. We do not collect, store, or share any personal user data, passwords, or private information. 

### Permissions & Shizuku Usage
- **Shizuku / System Commands:** The app utilizes local shell execution via Shizuku or system-level permissions solely to apply device performance tweaks (such as network and RAM optimizations) requested explicitly by the user on their own device.
- **Installed Packages:** The app reads installed package information locally on your device only to allow you to select and launch your target games from the Hub interface. This data never leaves your device.

### Security
We value your trust in providing us your information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable.

### Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
